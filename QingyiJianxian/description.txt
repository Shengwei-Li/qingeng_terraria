青衣剑仙：一个水墨风格的剑仙 Boss 模组。肉山之后可以挑战，难度与猪龙鱼公爵相当。
剑仙不会被杀死：接下他的最后一剑，战斗结束，他留下礼物离开。

■ 怎么遇到他
  · 打败骷髅王后，白天在地表有机会遇到他坐在云上喝酒（每天最多一次，天黑或附近没人时离开）
  · 困难模式之前攻击他：他会用剑气把你赶走，越打伤害越高，可能致死
  · 困难模式之后攻击他两次：开始战斗
  · 第一次战胜后获得"一壶酒"，之后可以随时召唤他

■ 战斗
  · 第一阶段：一剑横江、御剑术、落叶成剑
  · 第二阶段（生命 60%）：天空变成水墨山水；瞬身连斩、剑河、剑阵
  · 第三阶段（生命 25%）：天地变成空白，天上出现他的法相；万剑悬空、一笔画地、剑气纵横、醉剑
  · 最后一剑（生命 5%）：无法躲开，只能接下
  · 预警会逐阶段减少，第三阶段只能看剑尖寒光和动作
  · 六成以上的伤害来自剑类武器时获胜，他会额外认可你，多给一份礼物
  · 用清月剑攻击他，他会夺走清月剑，改用双剑

■ 掉落
  · 清月剑：三段连击加剑气，右键飞剑
  · 剑穗（饰品）：剑类伤害与暴击提升，暴击留下墨痕
  · 青云药酒：回复 200 生命，附带增益
  · 御剑诀：云坐骑
  · 清流剑符：消耗品，朝鼠标方向放出剑河，任何职业可用
  · 云纹绡：制作青衫套装（剑袍、发冠、云履），套装效果为冲刺、剑意叠层、罡气护体
  · 宝藏袋、纪念杯、大师模式遗物、斗笠

当前为测试版（0.5.1），配乐暂用原版曲目。反馈：https://github.com/Shengwei-Li/qingeng_terraria

The Azure-Clad Sword Immortal: an ink-wash style xianxia boss mod. Fightable after the Wall of Flesh, about as hard as Duke Fishron.
He can't be killed. Take his final stroke and the fight ends; he leaves you gifts and departs.

■ Finding him
  · After Skeletron, you may find him drinking on a cloud on the surface during the day (at most once a day; he leaves at night or when no one is near)
  · Attack him before hardmode and he drives you off with sword-qi that grows stronger each time and can kill
  · Hit him twice in hardmode to start the fight
  · Your first victory gives you the Cloudbrew Gourd, which summons him any time after that

■ The fight
  · Phase 1: sword dashes, flying swords, falling leaves
  · Phase 2 (60% life): the sky turns into an ink-wash landscape; blink slashes, a river of blades, a ring of blades
  · Phase 3 (25% life): the world turns blank and his giant likeness appears in the sky; hanging swords, a giant brushstroke, sweeping sword-qi, drunken blades
  · The final stroke (5% life): it can't be dodged, only taken
  · Warnings shrink each phase; in phase 3 you only get the glint of his sword and his stance
  · Win with at least 60% of your damage from swords and he acknowledges you with an extra gift
  · Attack him with Clearmoon and he takes it back and fights with two swords

■ Drops
  · Clearmoon: three-hit combo with sword-qi, right-click to throw
  · Inkwake Tassel (accessory): sword damage and crit, crits leave ink marks
  · Azure Cloud Draught: restores 200 life and grants a buff
  · Litany of Swordflight: cloud mount
  · Talisman of the Lingering Stream: consumable sword-qi river aimed at the cursor, usable by any class
  · Cloudweave Silk: crafts the Wayfarer set (robe, crown, boots) with a dash, sword-will stacks and a guarding effect
  · Treasure bag, trophy, Master Mode relic, bamboo hat
