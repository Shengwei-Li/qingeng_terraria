云上有人倚醉饮酒，青衣如水。
你若不扰他，他便不理你。

【青衣剑仙】一位水墨风格的剑仙 Boss，肉后即可挑战，定位与猪龙鱼公爵相当。
他不会被杀死：问剑，点到为止。接下他的最后一剑，他便赠剑离去。

■ 初遇
  · 打败骷髅王后，白天的地表偶尔会遇到他倚在云上喝酒（每天最多一次）
  · 肉前去惹他：他懒得起身，拂袖一道剑气把你赶走，不知进退可能真的会死
  · 肉后惹他第二下：他起身拔剑，问剑开始

■ 三个阶段
  · 问剑：一剑横江、御剑术、落叶成剑
  · 剑意：天地化作水墨山水；瞬身连斩、剑河、剑阵
  · 领域：天地空白，天上浮现他的法相；万剑悬空、一笔画地、剑气纵横、醉剑
  · 最后一剑：人剑合一，一剑开天——只能接，不能躲

■ 以剑问剑 · 夺剑
  · 用剑类武器打赢，他会认可你
  · 拿他赠的清月剑去打他？他会把剑夺走，换成双剑

■ 装备
  · 清月剑（三段连斩，月牙剑气）、剑穗、青云药酒、御剑诀（云坐骑）
  · 青衫套装：用他留下的云纹绡织成，踏云冲刺、剑意叠层、罡气护体
  · 专家宝藏袋、纪念杯、大师遗物、面具

Upon a cloud one lounges, wine in hand, robes the hue of clear water.
Trouble him not, and he will pay thee no heed.

The Azure-Clad Sword Immortal is an ink-wash xianxia boss, fightable after the Wall of Flesh — a challenge on par with Duke Fishron.
He cannot be slain: this is a questioning of blades, not a killing. Receive his final stroke, and he will leave thee his sword and drift away.

■ The Meeting
  · After Skeletron, thou mayest find him drinking upon a cloud above the surface by day (at most once a day)
  · Disturb him before hardmode, and a lazy flick of his sleeve will drive thee off — persist, and it may be thy death
  · Strike him twice in hardmode, and he rises and draws

■ Three Phases
  · The Questioning Blade, Sword-Will, and The Unwritten World — ink-wash skies, a colossal likeness of the Immortal, rivers and rings of blades
  · The Final Stroke: man and sword made one; it cannot be dodged, only received

■ Blade Answers Blade · The Blade Reclaimed
  · Win with swords, and he shall acknowledge thee
  · Dare to strike him with the very blade he gave thee? He takes it back, and fights with two

■ Gear
  · Clearmoon, the Inkwake Tassel, Azure Cloud Draught, the Litany of Swordflight (a cloud mount)
  · The Azure Wayfarer set, woven from the Cloudweave Silk he leaves behind
  · Treasure bag, trophy, relic and mask
